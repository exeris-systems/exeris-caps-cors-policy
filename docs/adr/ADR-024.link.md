# ADR-024 — Capability Composition Model (`@Provides` / `@Requires` / Build-Time Validation) (link stub)

**Authoritative copy:** [`exeris-docs/adr/ADR-024-capability-composition-model.md`](https://github.com/exeris-systems/exeris-docs/blob/main/adr/ADR-024-capability-composition-model.md)

ADR-024 is a **cross-repo** decision owned by `exeris-docs` (platform scope). It defines the four
capability annotations, the build-time resolution of the composition graph, and the five validation
predicates a build must satisfy before a cap set is composable.

## Why this stub exists

This repository is the **first capability in the ecosystem**, and `CorsPolicyModule` is the whole of
its realization of ADR-024: one `@CapabilityModule`, one `@Provides(service, version)`, no
`@Requires`, no `@CapabilityLifecycle`. There is no larger implementation here to read the decision
out of — the declaration is nine lines and the reasoning lives upstream. Anyone changing that
declaration is changing this repo's ADR-024 conformance, whether or not they think of it that way.

## When to consult ADR-024 from this repo

- **Any edit to `CorsPolicyModule`.** The annotations are `@Retention(SOURCE)`, so they are erased at
  `javac` and the build-time pipeline is their only possible consumer — there is no runtime behaviour
  to test the declaration against. ADR-024 is the specification; the manifest is the evidence.
- **Adding a `@Requires`.** ADR-024 makes `initOrder` a topological sort of the `@Requires` DAG. An
  empty or speculative edge is not inert: it changes the derived order for *every* SKU that includes
  this cap. This cap requires nothing, and that is a claim about its decision function being total
  over its inputs, not an unfilled blank.
- **Changing `@Provides(version = …)`.** The version is a semver contract on the service, not a
  label on the jar. `ROADMAP.md` 0.2.0 records the one rule under which the planned reshape of
  `CorsPolicy` stays inside `1.0.0`, together with the condition that expires it — read that before
  concluding either that a bump is required or that it is not.
- **Validation predicate 4 (host-runtime isolation).** Its build-time realization is the cap-tier
  Wall; see [`ADR-055.link.md`](ADR-055.link.md). ADR-024 states what must be true, ADR-055 states
  what the scan actually enforces, and the two have drifted once already.
- **Obligations 8a / 8b** (lifecycle hooks, canonical content binding) are realized by
  `exeris-sdk-composition-lifecycle` and `exeris-sdk-composition-spec`. This cap declares no
  lifecycle, so only the binding half applies — see [`ADR-053.link.md`](ADR-053.link.md).

## Engineering status

ADR-024 is **ACCEPTED** (2026-05-13). `ROADMAP.md` 0.2.0 plans ADR-085 in this repository, which
amends HLA §3.2 and stands in the ADR-024 lineage as the first *cap-contract* decision: ADR-024
governs how a cap declares its services, ADR-085 will govern what a cap's service surface may look
like. When ADR-085 is accepted, this stub should gain a forward reference to it.
